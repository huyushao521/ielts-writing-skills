# IELTS Task 2 Essay Types

## 1. Opinion (观点类)

**Prompt patterns:**
- "Do you agree or disagree?"
- "To what extent do you agree or disagree?"
- "What is your opinion?"
- "Do you think...?"

**Structure (4 paragraphs recommended):**
```
Introduction:
  - Paraphrase the prompt
  - Thesis statement: clear position (agree/disagree/partially agree)

Body 1: First reason + evidence
  - Topic sentence (main argument)
  - Explanation
  - Example
  - Concluding sentence

Body 2: Second reason + evidence
  - Topic sentence (supporting argument)
  - Explanation
  - Example
  - Concluding sentence

Conclusion:
  - Restate position
  - Summarize key points
  - Final thought (optional)
```

**Signal phrases for thesis:**
- "I completely agree with the view that..."
- "I firmly disagree with the assertion that..."
- "While I acknowledge that..., I firmly believe that..."
- "This essay will argue that..."

## 2. Discussion (讨论类)

**Prompt patterns:**
- "Discuss both views and give your opinion."
- "Some people think X, while others believe Y. Discuss both sides."

**Structure (4-5 paragraphs):**
```
Introduction:
  - Paraphrase both views
  - State own opinion

Body 1: First view (arguments for)
  - Topic sentence: summarize this view
  - Explain why some people hold this view
  - Example

Body 2: Second view (arguments against)
  - Topic sentence: summarize this view
  - Explain why others hold this view
  - Example

[Body 3: Own opinion (if separate paragraph needed)]

Conclusion:
  - Summarize both views
  - Restate own opinion with final reasoning
```

**Signal phrases:**
- "Proponents of this view argue that..."
- "On the other hand, opponents contend that..."
- "While some advocate..., others maintain..."
- "From my perspective, I am more inclined to..."

## 3. Advantage/Disadvantage (优缺点类)

**Prompt patterns:**
- "Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?"
- "What are the advantages and disadvantages?"
- "Is this a positive or negative development?"

**Structure (4 paragraphs):**
```
Introduction:
  - Paraphrase the situation/trend
  - State whether positives or negatives are stronger (thesis)

Body 1: Advantages
  - Topic sentence
  - 1-2 key benefits with examples

Body 2: Disadvantages
  - Topic sentence
  - 1-2 key drawbacks with examples

Conclusion:
  - Weigh both sides
  - Restate which is stronger
```

**Signal phrases:**
- "One significant advantage of... is that..."
- "A further benefit worth noting is..."
- "However, this approach is not without its drawbacks."
- "The primary disadvantage associated with... is..."
- "On balance, the benefits appear to outweigh the negatives."

## 4. Problem/Solution (问题解决类)

**Prompt patterns:**
- "What are the causes and solutions?"
- "What problems does this cause and what solutions can you suggest?"
- "Why is this happening and what measures can be taken?"

**Structure (4 paragraphs):**
```
Introduction:
  - Paraphrase the problem
  - State that causes and solutions will be discussed

Body 1: Causes/Problems
  - Topic sentence
  - 2 causes with explanations and examples

Body 2: Solutions
  - Topic sentence
  - 2 solutions (corresponding to the causes) with explanations

Conclusion:
  - Summarize causes and solutions
  - Final comment on feasibility
```

**Signal phrases:**
- "One of the primary causes of this phenomenon is..."
- "This can be attributed to..."
- "A contributing factor is..."
- "To address this issue, one effective measure would be..."
- "Another viable solution involves..."
- "Governments could tackle this by..."

## 5. Two-Part Question (双问题类)

**Prompt patterns:**
- Two separate questions, often "Why...? What...?" or "Is...? How...?"

**Structure (4 paragraphs):**
```
Introduction:
  - Paraphrase both questions
  - Brief thesis indicating answers

Body 1: Answer to Question 1
  - Topic sentence directly addressing Q1
  - Explanation + example

Body 2: Answer to Question 2
  - Topic sentence directly addressing Q2
  - Explanation + example

Conclusion:
  - Summarize answers to both questions
```

**Signal phrases:**
- "Regarding the first question,..."
- "In response to the second issue,..."
- "As for why this occurs,..."
- "Concerning how to address it,..."

## Common Topic Areas

- Education (education systems, university, subjects)
- Technology (internet, AI, social media, automation)
- Environment (climate change, pollution, conservation)
- Society & Culture (aging population, urbanization, traditions)
- Health (diet, exercise, mental health, healthcare)
- Economy & Work (remote work, job satisfaction, gig economy)
- Government & Policy (funding, regulation, taxation)
- Media & Advertising (fake news, advertising to children)
- Transport (public vs private, electric vehicles)
- Crime & Law (punishment vs rehabilitation, gun control)
