# IELTS Task 2 Scoring Rubric

The essay is scored on four criteria, each worth 25% of the Task 2 contribution.

## 1. Task Response (TR)

| Band | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 9 | Fully addresses all parts of the task. Presents a fully developed position with relevant, extended and supported ideas. |
| 8 | Sufficiently addresses all parts of the task. Presents a well-developed response with relevant, extended and supported ideas. |
| 7 | Addresses all parts of the task. Presents a clear position throughout. Presents, extends and supports main ideas, but there may be a tendency to over-generalize or there may be a lack of focus. |
| 6 | Addresses all parts of the task although some parts may be more fully covered than others. Presents a relevant position, although conclusions may become unclear or repetitive. Presents relevant main ideas but some may be inadequately developed or unclear. |
| 5 | Addresses the task only partially. Expresses a position but development is not always clear. Presents some main ideas but these are limited and not sufficiently developed; there may be irrelevant detail. |
| 4 | Responds to the task only in a minimal way or the answer is tangential. Position may be unclear. Presents few ideas, which are largely undeveloped or irrelevant. |
| 3 | Does not adequately address any part of the task. Does not express a clear position. Presents few ideas, which are largely undeveloped or irrelevant. |
| 2 | Barely responds to the task. Does not express a position. May attempt to present one or two ideas but with no development. |
| 1 | Answer is completely unrelated to the task. |

**Key TR indicators for scoring:**
- Does the essay answer the actual question asked?
- Is the position clear and consistent?
- Are ideas well-supported with examples?
- Are there enough relevant ideas?
- Is the word count met (250+)?

## 2. Coherence and Cohesion (CC)

| Band | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 9 | Uses cohesion in such a way that it attracts no attention. Skilfully manages paragraphing. Uses referencing and substitution seamlessly. |
| 8 | Messages and ideas follow each other logically. All aspects of cohesion are well-managed. Uses paragraphing sufficiently and appropriately. |
| 7 | Logically organizes information and ideas. Clear progression throughout. Uses a range of cohesive devices appropriately although there may be some under-/over-use. Presents a clear central topic within each paragraph. |
| 6 | Arranges information and ideas coherently. Uses cohesive devices effectively, but cohesion within and/or between paragraphs may be faulty or mechanical. May not always use referencing clearly or appropriately. Uses paragraphing, but may not be logical. |
| 5 | Presents information with some organization but may be generally undeveloped. Relationships between ideas may be unclear or repetitive. May not write in paragraphs or paragraphing may be inadequate. |
| 4 | Presents information and ideas but with limited organization. May not progress logically. Uses limited cohesive devices. May be repetitive due to lack of referencing and substitution. |
| 3 | Does not organize ideas logically. Uses very limited cohesive devices. May not use paragraphs at all. |
| 2 | Has very little control of organizational features. |
| 1 | Communication is impossible due to lack of language. |

**Key CC indicators for scoring:**
- Is there a logical flow from introduction to conclusion?
- Are cohesive devices used naturally (not forced/memorized)?
- Does each paragraph have a clear central topic?
- Is referencing (this, these, it, such) used correctly?
- Is paragraphing logical?

## 3. Lexical Resource (LR)

| Band | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 9 | Uses a wide range of vocabulary with very natural and sophisticated control of lexical features. Rare errors in spelling/word formation. |
| 8 | Uses a wide range of vocabulary fluently and flexibly. Uses uncommon lexical items with awareness of style and collocation. Occasional errors in word choice/spelling. |
| 7 | Uses a sufficient range of vocabulary to allow some flexibility and precision. Uses less common lexical items with some awareness of style and collocation. May produce occasional errors in word choice, spelling and word formation. |
| 6 | Uses an adequate range of vocabulary for the task. Attempts to use less common vocabulary but with some inaccuracy. Makes some errors in spelling and word formation, but communication is not impeded. |
| 5 | Uses a limited range of vocabulary, but generally adequate for the task. May make noticeable errors in spelling and word formation that may cause some difficulty for the reader. Uses repetitive language. |
| 4 | Uses a narrow range of vocabulary. Noticeable errors in spelling and word formation may cause difficulty for the reader. |
| 3 | Uses a very limited range of vocabulary. Errors in spelling and word formation are frequent and impede communication. |
| 2 | Uses an extremely limited range of vocabulary; insufficient for the task. |
| 1 | Can only use a few isolated words. |

**Key LR indicators for scoring:**
- Is there a range of vocabulary (not repeating same words)?
- Are less common words used correctly (collocation)?
- Is spelling accurate?
- Are word forms correct?
- Are idiomatic expressions used naturally (not forced)?

## 4. Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA)

| Band | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 9 | Uses a wide range of structures with full flexibility and precise control. Rare minor errors. |
| 8 | Uses a wide range of structures. Majority of sentences are error-free. Makes only very occasional errors. |
| 7 | Uses a variety of complex structures. Produces frequent error-free sentences. Good control of grammar and punctuation; makes a few errors. |
| 6 | Uses a mix of simple and complex sentence forms. Makes some errors in grammar and punctuation but they rarely impede communication. |
| 5 | Uses only a limited range of complex structures. Sentences may contain frequent errors. Punctuation may be faulty; errors can cause some difficulty for the reader. |
| 4 | Uses only a very limited range of structures. Rare complex sentences (faulty). Frequent errors in grammar and punctuation. |
| 3 | Uses basic sentence forms only. Attempts complex sentences but with severe errors. Errors are frequent and impede meaning. |
| 2 | Uses sentence forms but with very limited control. Cannot produce complex sentences. |
| 1 | Cannot use sentence forms at all except in memorized phrases. |

**Key GRA indicators for scoring:**
- Is there a mix of simple, compound, and complex sentences?
- Are error-free sentences frequent?
- Is punctuation correct?
- Are verb tenses accurate and appropriate?
- Are articles, prepositions, and subject-verb agreement correct?
- Are relative clauses, conditionals, passive voice used?

## Overall Band Calculation

Overall = average of (TR + CC + LR + GRA), rounded to nearest 0.5 or whole band.

Example:
- TR: 7, CC: 7, LR: 6.5, GRA: 6.5 → (7+7+6.5+6.5)/4 = 6.75 → rounds to 7.0

**Rounding rule:** If the average ends in .25 or .75, round up to the next half band; otherwise round to nearest 0.5.
