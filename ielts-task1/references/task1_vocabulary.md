# Band 7+ Vocabulary for IELTS Task 1

## 1. Trend Vocabulary (for Line Charts & Time-based Data)

### Upward Movement

| Band 5 | Band 7+ |
|--------|---------|
| go up | rise, increase, grow, climb, ascend |
| go up a lot | surge, soar, skyrocket, escalate sharply |
| go up a little | inch up, edge upward, rise marginally |
| increase | experience an upward trend, show an upward trajectory |

**Adverbs of degree:**
- sharply, steeply, dramatically, considerably, substantially (large increase)
- moderately, gradually, steadily (medium increase)
- slightly, marginally, minimally, marginally (small increase)

**Noun forms:**
- a sharp rise, a dramatic increase, a steady growth, a gradual climb

### Downward Movement

| Band 5 | Band 7+ |
|--------|---------|
| go down | fall, decline, decrease, drop, descend |
| go down a lot | plummet, plunge, nosedive, slump |
| go down a little | dip, edge downward, decline marginally |
| decrease | experience a downward trend, show a decline |

**Adverbs of degree:**
- sharply, steeply, dramatically, drastically (large decrease)
- moderately, gradually, steadily (medium decrease)
- slightly, marginally, minimally (small decrease)

**Noun forms:**
- a sharp fall, a dramatic decline, a steady decrease, a gradual drop

### Fluctuation and Stability

| Band 5 | Band 7+ |
|--------|---------|
| go up and down | fluctuate, oscillate, vary |
| stay the same | remain stable, plateau, level off, remain constant |
| stay at a level | hover around, maintain at, persist at |
| change a lot | experience significant volatility |

**Sample sentences:**
- "The figures fluctuated between [X] and [Y] throughout the period."
- "After [year], the value plateaued at approximately [X]."
- "The data remained relatively stable, hovering around the [X] mark."
- "The [variable] experienced considerable volatility, ranging from [X] to [Y]."

### Peaks and Troughs

| Expression | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| reach a peak of | "reached a peak of [X] in [year]" |
| reach a high of | "hit a high of [X]" |
| reach a low of | "fell to a low of [X]" |
| bottom out at | "bottomed out at [X]" |
| hit a trough of | "hit a trough of [X] in [year]" |

## 2. Comparison Vocabulary (for Bar Charts, Tables, Pie Charts)

### Equality and Similarity

| Band 5 | Band 7+ |
|--------|---------|
| the same as | identical to, equal to, on par with |
| similar to | comparable to, analogous to, closely resembling |
| almost the same | approximately equal, roughly equivalent |

**Sample sentences:**
- "The figures for [A] and [B] were remarkably similar, at [X] and [Y] respectively."
- "[A] and [B] were on par with each other, both recording approximately [X]."
- "The proportion for [A] was roughly equivalent to that of [B]."

### Difference and Superiority

| Band 5 | Band 7+ |
|--------|---------|
| bigger than | greater than, exceeded, surpassed, outstripped |
| smaller than | less than, fell below, was dwarfed by |
| the biggest | the largest, the most significant, the predominant |
| the smallest | the smallest, the least, the minimal |
| much bigger | considerably larger, substantially higher, vastly greater |
| a little bigger | marginally higher, slightly greater, fractionally more |

**Sample sentences:**
- "[A] significantly outstripped [B], with figures of [X] and [Y] respectively."
- "The gap between [A] and [B] was substantial, with [A] being nearly [N] times greater."
- "[A] dwarfed [B] in terms of [metric], recording [X] compared to [B]'s mere [Y]."
- "There was a notable disparity between [A] and [B]."

### Proportions and Percentages (for Pie Charts)

| Band 5 | Band 7+ |
|--------|---------|
| most of | the majority of, the bulk of, the lion's share of |
| a big part | a significant proportion, a substantial portion |
| a small part | a minor fraction, a negligible proportion |
| half | exactly one-half, 50% of the total |
| a quarter | one-fourth, 25% of the overall figure |

**Fractions and approximations:**
- approximately, roughly, just over, just under, nearly, almost, slightly more than, slightly less than
- one-third, two-thirds, three-quarters, one-fifth

**Sample sentences:**
- "[A] accounted for the lion's share at [X]%."
- "[A] constituted the majority, making up [X]% of the total."
- "The remaining [X]% was distributed among [A], [B], and [C]."
- "[A] and [B] combined represented over half the total at [X]%."
- "The proportion of [A] was roughly three times that of [B]."

## 4. Introduction Paraphrasing

### Templates

| Chart Type | Template |
|-----------|----------|
| Line chart | "The line graph illustrates/illustrates how [variable] changed over [time period]." |
| Bar chart | "The bar chart compares [variable] across [N] categories [in year]." |
| Pie chart | "The pie chart shows the breakdown/proportion of [variable] into [N] categories [in year]." |
| Table | "The table provides information about [variable] across [categories] [in year]." |
| Process | "The diagram illustrates the process by which [product] is manufactured/produced." |
| Map | "The maps illustrate the changes that took place in [location] between [year 1] and [year 2]." |
| Multiple | "The [chart 1] and [chart 2] provide data on [topic] [in year]." |

### Paraphrasing the prompt verbs

| Original | Paraphrase |
|----------|------------|
| shows | illustrates, depicts, presents, displays, provides information about |
| compares | contrasts, sets side by side |
| describes | outlines, details, explains |
| the number of | the quantity of, the figure for |
| the percentage of | the proportion of, the share of |
| changes in | variations in, shifts in, fluctuations in |

## 5. Grammar Patterns for Band 7+

### Complex Sentences

1. **While/whereas contrast:**
   - "While [A] increased, [B] decreased over the same period."

2. **Although concession:**
   - "Although [A] showed an overall upward trend, it experienced a temporary decline in [year]."

3. **Having + past participle:**
   - "Having peaked at [X] in [year], [A] subsequently declined to [Y]."

4. **Which relative clause:**
   - "[A], which had been the highest in [year], was overtaken by [B] in [year]."

5. **Passive voice (especially for process):**
   - "The material is then transferred to [location], where it is [processed]."

6. **Participle clauses:**
   - "Starting at [X] in [year], [A] rose steadily to reach [Y] by [year]."
   - "Peaking at [X] in [year], the figure then declined."

7. **Not only... but also:**
   - "Not only did [A] increase, but [B] also showed a similar upward trend."

8. **It is clear/evident that:**
   - "It is evident from the chart that [overall observation]."

## 6. Words and Phrases to Avoid

| Avoid | Reason | Use Instead |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| As we can see | Too informal | "As can be seen from the chart" / "It is evident that" |
| Look at the chart | Imperative, informal | "The chart shows that" |
| I think / In my opinion | Task 1 is objective | (Delete entirely) |
| To conclude | Task 1 has overview, not conclusion | "Overall" / "In general" |
| A lot of | Too informal | "A significant number of" / "A considerable amount of" |
| Big / small | Too basic | "Substantial" / "minimal" / "considerable" / "negligible" |
| The graph tells us | Charts don't "tell" | "The graph indicates/shows/demonstrates" |
| Firstly, secondly | Too mechanical for Task 1 | "Turning to..." / "Regarding..." |
| In a nutshell | Too informal | "Overall" / "In general" |
