## Sample 1: Line Chart — Internet Users (Band 8.0)

**Prompt:** The line graph below shows the percentage of people using the internet in three countries from 2000 to 2020. Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

**Data (described):**
- Country A: 2000: 15% → 2010: 50% → 2020: 85%
- Country B: 2000: 5% → 2010: 35% → 2020: 70%
- Country C: 2000: 25% → 2010: 60% → 2020: 78%

**Sample Report:**

The line graph illustrates the proportion of internet users in three countries — Country A, Country B, and Country C — over a twenty-year period from 2000 to 2020.

Overall, all three countries experienced a significant upward trend in internet usage throughout the period. Country C maintained the highest percentage for the majority of the period, while Country B consistently recorded the lowest figures, despite showing the most dramatic growth.

In 2000, Country C had the highest proportion of internet users at 25%, followed by Country A at 15%, while Country B had the lowest at just 5%. Over the next decade, all three countries saw substantial increases. Country C reached 60% by 2010, maintaining its leading position, while Country A grew to 50%, nearly closing the gap. Country B, although still the lowest, more than tripled its figure to 35%.

Between 2010 and 2020, the growth continued but at varying rates. Country A overtook Country C around 2015, ultimately reaching the highest figure of 85% by 2020. Country C's growth slowed, ending at 78%. Country B, despite starting from the lowest base, showed the most remarkable increase, rising to 70% and significantly narrowing the gap with the other two nations.

*(214 words)*

**Band 8.0 features:**
- Clear overview paragraph with main trends identified
- Strategic data selection (not every year described)
- Excellent vocabulary: "proportion," "upward trend," "triple," "narrowing the gap"
- Complex grammar: relative clauses, passive voice, participle clauses
- Logical paragraphing: Intro → Overview → Details 1 (2000-2010) → Details 2 (2010-2020)
- Crossing point identified ("overtook around 2015")

## Sample 2: Bar Chart — Energy Consumption (Band 7.5)

**Prompt:** The bar chart below shows the energy consumption in five countries in 2020. Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

**Data (described):**
- Country A: 8,000 GWh
- Country B: 6,500 GWh
- Country C: 5,000 GWh
- Country D: 3,500 GWh
- Country E: 2,000 GWh

**Sample Report:**

The bar chart compares the energy consumption of five countries — A, B, C, D, and E — in the year 2020, measured in gigawatt-hours (GWh).

Overall, Country A consumed significantly more energy than any other nation, while Country E had the lowest consumption by a considerable margin. There was a clear gradient, with each country consuming progressively less energy than the one preceding it.

Country A recorded the highest energy consumption at 8,000 GWh, which was approximately 1,500 GWh more than the second-highest, Country B, at 6,500 GWh. Together, these two nations accounted for the largest share of the total energy consumed among the five countries.

Country C ranked in the middle with 5,000 GWh, followed by Country D at 3,500 GWh. The figures for these two countries were notably lower than those of A and B, yet they still consumed considerably more than Country E. Country E recorded the lowest consumption at just 2,000 GWh, which was a mere quarter of Country A's figure, highlighting a substantial disparity between the highest and lowest consumers.

*(194 words)*

**Band 7.5 features:**
- Clear overview
- Good data selection and comparison
- Vocabulary: "considerable margin," "gradient," "substantial disparity"
- Good use of fractions ("a mere quarter")
- Could improve: more complex sentence structures

## Sample 3: Pie Chart — Budget Allocation (Band 7.5)

**Prompt:** The pie charts below show the changes in a household's monthly budget between 2000 and 2020. Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

**Data (described):**
- 2000: Food 35%, Housing 25%, Transport 15%, Entertainment 10%, Savings 10%, Other 5%
- 2020: Food 20%, Housing 35%, Transport 15%, Entertainment 15%, Savings 10%, Other 5%

**Sample Report:**

The pie charts illustrate the changes in a household's monthly budget allocation across six categories between the years 2000 and 2020.

Overall, the most significant shift was the reduction in the proportion spent on food and the corresponding increase in housing expenditure. Transport and savings remained unchanged, while entertainment saw a moderate increase.

In 2000, food constituted the largest category at 35% of the total budget, followed by housing at 25%. By 2020, these positions had reversed, with housing becoming the dominant expense at 35%, while food's share dropped to 20%. This represents a 15-percentage-point decrease for food and a 10-percentage-point increase for housing.

The proportion allocated to entertainment grew from 10% to 15%, indicating an increased spending on leisure activities. Meanwhile, transport expenditure remained stable at 15% in both years, as did the savings rate, which stayed constant at 10%. The "Other" category was also unchanged at 5%. These stable categories suggest that the household prioritized consistent spending on travel and savings despite the overall budget restructuring.

*(188 words)*

**Band 7.5 features:**
- Clear overview identifying the most significant change
- Good comparison across two pie charts
- Precise data reporting with percentage points
- Vocabulary: "constituted," "dominant expense," "budget restructuring"
- Logical grouping: changed categories together, stable categories together

## Sample 4: Table — Population Statistics (Band 7.0)

**Prompt:** The table below gives information about the population, GDP, and life expectancy in four countries in 2020. Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

**Data (described):**
| Country | Population (millions) | GDP per capita ($) | Life Expectancy (years) |
|---------|---------------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| A | 1,400 | 10,000 | 77 |
| B | 330 | 60,000 | 80 |
| C | 1,350 | 2,000 | 70 |
| D | 65 | 45,000 | 82 |

**Sample Report:**

The table provides data on the population, gross domestic product per capita, and life expectancy for four countries in 2020.

Overall, Countries A and C had by far the largest populations, while Country B had the highest GDP per capita. Country D, despite having the smallest population, recorded the highest life expectancy, suggesting that population size does not necessarily correlate with economic development or health outcomes.

Countries A and C dominated in terms of population, with 1,400 million and 1,350 million respectively. In contrast, Countries B and D had considerably smaller populations at 330 million and 65 million. Despite this, Country B's GDP per capita was the highest at $60,000, which was six times that of Country A ($10,000) and thirty times that of Country C ($2,000). Country D recorded a GDP per capita of $45,000, the second highest.

Regarding life expectancy, Country D led with 82 years, followed closely by Country B at 80 years. Countries A and C recorded lower figures at 77 and 70 years respectively. Notably, Country C, which had the second-largest population, had both the lowest GDP per capita and the lowest life expectancy among the four nations.

*(204 words)*

**Band 7.0 features:**
- Clear overview with insight ("population size does not necessarily correlate...")
- Strategic data selection across three metrics
- Good use of comparison language ("six times," "thirty times")
- Logical grouping by metric
- Could improve: more varied sentence structures, less mechanical listing

## Sample 5: Process Diagram — Chocolate Production (Band 8.0)

**Prompt:** The diagram below shows the stages of chocolate production. Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

**Stages (described):**
1. Cacao pods harvested → 2. White cacao beans extracted → 3. Beans fermented → 4. Beans dried in sun → 5. Beans roasted → 6. Beans winnowed (shell removed) → 7. Beans ground into paste → 8. Cocoa butter pressed out → 9. Cocoa powder + sugar + milk mixed → 10. Chocolate molded → 11. Final chocolate product

**Sample Report:**

The diagram illustrates the process by which chocolate is manufactured, from the harvesting of cacao pods to the production of the final chocolate product. The process consists of approximately ten stages, which can be broadly divided into three phases: preparation of raw beans, processing into cocoa, and manufacturing of chocolate.

The process begins with the harvesting of cacao pods, from which white cacao beans are extracted. These beans then undergo fermentation, a crucial step that develops the chocolate flavor. Once fermented, the beans are dried in the sun, after which they are roasted to further enhance their flavor profile.

In the next phase, the roasted beans are winnowed, a process that removes their outer shells. The inner nibs are then ground into a paste known as chocolate liquor. This paste is subsequently pressed to extract cocoa butter, leaving behind cocoa solids that are processed into cocoa powder.

In the final stage, the cocoa powder is combined with sugar and milk, and the cocoa butter is reintroduced to create the chocolate mixture. This mixture is then poured into molds, where it solidifies to form the final chocolate product ready for distribution.

*(203 words)*

**Band 8.0 features:**
- Excellent overview grouping stages into phases
- Consistent use of passive voice throughout
- Sequencing vocabulary: "begins with," "once fermented," "after which," "subsequently," "in the final stage"
- Precise vocabulary: "winnowed," "chocolate liquor," "solidifies"
- Clear logical progression
- Good paraphrasing of process stages

## Sample 6: Map — Village Development (Band 7.5)

**Prompt:** The maps below show the village of Eastborough in 1990 and 2020. Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

**Changes (described):**
- 1990: Small village with farms, a river, a few houses, a primary school, a church
- 2020: Expanded residential area (houses replaced farms), new shopping center, new secondary school, river diverted, church retained, primary school expanded

**Sample Report:**

The maps illustrate the changes that have taken place in the village of Eastborough between 1990 and 2020.

Overall, the village has been transformed from a small rural settlement into a more developed suburban area. The most significant changes include the replacement of farmland with residential housing, the construction of new facilities, and the diversion of the river.

In 1990, the village consisted of a small number of houses clustered around a primary school and a church in the center, with extensive farmland occupying the eastern part of the area. The river flowed from north to south through the western section of the village.

By 2020, the farmland had been entirely replaced by a large residential development, significantly expanding the village's housing stock. A new shopping center was constructed in the southern part of the village, and a secondary school was built to the east, supplementing the expanded primary school. The river was diverted to the west, creating space for the new developments. Notably, the church in the center of the village remained unchanged, serving as a point of continuity amid the extensive transformations.

*(194 words)*

**Band 7.5 features:**
- Clear overview summarizing the overall transformation
- Systematic comparison using spatial organization
- Appropriate tense usage (past for 1990, past perfect for changes, past for 2020 description)
- Vocabulary: "transformed," "suburban area," "extensive transformations"
- Retained feature noted ("church remained unchanged")
- Could improve: more directional precision, more complex comparisons
